How events are grouped
Failures and route changes join one incident when they share a node or group and fall within 5 minutes of each other. A recovery joins the incident that holds its path.
Syslog (severity 5 or worse) and bandwidth tests within 5 minutes of an incident are listed under it as nearby. That is timing, not proof of cause: the hub has no map from a device name to a node.
A path already failing when the range begins gets one event at its first sample. “On a new route” means a path change in the 15 minutes before the moment shown.
